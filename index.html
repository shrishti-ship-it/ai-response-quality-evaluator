    Evaluation framework

    This prototype uses five dimensions, each scored from 0 to 5.

    • Relevance: Does the answer address the task?
    • Completeness: Does it adequately answer the task?
    • Instruction-following: Does it respect the requested constraints?
    • Evidence quality: How well do claims align with supplied reference text?
    • Safety: Are there apparent safety concerns?

    AI-generated assessments can be mistaken or inconsistent. Scores are estimates, not validated measures of quality or proof of factual accuracy. Human review remains necessary.
